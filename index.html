Privacy Policy

How information is handled when you use our application or services

Effective date: 18 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Enaya Boutique (“we”, “our”, or “us”) may collect, use, and protect information when you use our application or related services. Please read it carefully.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may receive information you provide, such as your name, phone number, appointment details, selected service, and messages you send us. If you browse the gallery, basic technical information may also be processed by your device or service providers.

Please provide only information needed to make or manage an appointment. Do not submit sensitive personal information unless it is specifically necessary.

2. How We Use Information

  • To receive, arrange, and manage appointment requests.
  • To respond to questions and customer support requests.
  • To provide and maintain the app and its gallery or related features.
  • To help protect the service, troubleshoot issues, and prevent misuse.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the app, such as hosting or database providers, or disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, and security. We aim to limit sharing to what is reasonably necessary.

4. Storage and Security

Information may be stored using digital service providers. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no online service or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed completely secure. Access to information should be limited to people who need it to operate the service.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained for as long as reasonably needed to manage appointments, provide the service, resolve disputes, or meet legal requirements. To ask about access, correction, or deletion of your information, contact the boutique using the contact option below. Some records may need to be retained where required by law.

6. Children’s Privacy

The app is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate permission from a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so we can review the request.

7. Third-Party Services

The app may rely on third-party services for hosting, database functionality, or links. Those providers may process data under their own terms and privacy policies. We encourage users to review the policies of any third-party service they interact with.

8. Your Choices

You may choose not to provide optional information, although some app features—such as making an appointment—may not work without the relevant details. You can contact us to request correction or deletion of information, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our app, practices, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
